Programme overview

One place to run Dangote's mentorship programme, end to end.

A bilingual, AI-assisted portal that takes the programme from spreadsheets and paperwork to a single, measurable system — from the first import of names to the certificate at the finish.

9-month journey Bilingual — English & French AI-assisted, human-approved Built for ~120 mentors & ~300 mentees

The idea in one minute

What the portal is

Today, running a mentorship programme means chasing forms, matching people by hand, and hoping pairs stay active. The portal replaces all of that with one guided system. Every participant follows the same clear journey; administrators see exactly what's happening; and an AI assistant does the heavy lifting — drafting, summarising, translating, and flagging problems — while people stay in control of every real decision.

Phase 1

Get everyone in, and matched

Bring in mentor and mentee details, clean and check the data, let the system suggest the best pairings, and have an administrator review and approve them before anyone is matched.

  • Import from Excel, CSV or Google Forms
  • Automatic checks flag missing or messy information
  • Smart matching ranks the best mentor for each mentee
  • An admin approves — the system never matches on its own
Phase 2

Run the mentorship, month by month

Once pairs are set, the portal carries them through the whole nine months: agreements, goals, sessions, reviews and recognition — with reminders and reporting throughout.

  • Signed mentoring & confidentiality agreements
  • Goal setting, session logs and action items
  • Mid-term and final reviews
  • Private messaging and progress reporting

The spine of the product

The nine-month journey

Every participant sees the same simple progress line across the top of their screen. It shows where they are, what they've finished, and the one thing to do next — so nobody gets lost, and administrators can see the whole cohort's progress at a glance.

1

Profile

Each person completes their profile — background, skills, goals, and preferred language.

2

Training

Mentors and mentees complete their onboarding training before being matched.

3

Matched

The system suggests pairings, an admin approves, and both people accept the match.

4

Agreement

The pair digitally signs a mentoring agreement and a confidentiality agreement.

5

Goals

The mentee sets clear, measurable goals — with an AI coach to help sharpen them — and the mentor approves.

6

Sessions

The pair meets regularly. Each session is logged, summarised, and turned into follow-up actions.

7

Mid-term review

Both sides reflect halfway through, so the programme can spot what's working and what isn't.

8

Final review

A closing review captures results, growth, and lessons for the next cohort.

★

Certificate

The mentee completes the programme and earns recognition for the journey.

What it does

The features, in plain terms

Everything a programme needs to run — grouped by what people actually do.

Intake & data checks

Upload participant lists from a spreadsheet or form. The portal cleans the data and flags problems in plain language — "this mentor has no language selected", "these five people look like duplicates" — so an admin fixes them before anyone becomes a profile.

Smart matching

The system scores every possible mentor–mentee pairing on language, skills, career goals, experience and availability, then explains its reasoning in a sentence. An administrator reviews the ranked suggestions and approves or overrides — the match is always a human decision.

Agreements & e-signing

Each pair signs a mentoring agreement and a confidentiality agreement right in the portal, saved as PDFs for the record.

Goal setting with an AI coach

Mentees write goals; the AI coach rewrites vague ones into clear, measurable targets as a suggestion. The mentee edits, and the mentor approves — nothing is set without sign-off.

New

Session logs & mentor reports

After each meeting, the mentor records what was discussed and the actions agreed — the AI can tidy rough notes into a clean summary. Mentors can always re-open and re-read every report they've filed, and administrators now have a read-only view of every session report across the programme.

Meetings & calendar

Schedule sessions, see upcoming meetings, and keep a shared calendar for each pair — with reminders so meetings don't slip.

Reviews & programme health

Editable mid-term and final review forms, plus an AI monitor that quietly flags pairs that haven't met, stale goals, or missing logs — so problems surface early.

Private messaging

Each pair gets a private one-to-one chat with read receipts and a one-tap translate button. Message content stays private between the two people.

Dashboards for every role

Admins see totals and alerts; mentors see their mentees and to-dos; mentees see their goals and next steps; executives see programme-wide impact.

Bilingual, everywhere

The whole portal works in English and French, and no French speaker is ever forced to write in English. Any note, goal or message can be translated with one tap, and both versions are kept.

The intelligent layer

Eight AI assistants — you stay in charge

The rule:

The AI proposes; a person decides. It never matches a pair, approves a goal, or sends a newsletter on its own. Every real action is confirmed by an administrator, mentor or mentee.

1

Matching Assistant

Scores and explains the best pairings, flags weak or missing data.

2

Goal Coach

Turns vague goals into clear, measurable ones as a suggestion.

3

Session Assistant

Summarises notes, pulls out action items, drafts the next agenda.

4

Review Assistant

Analyses reviews, summarises progress, drafts the programme report.

5

Newsletter Assistant

Drafts updates from real activity for an admin to review and send.

6

Clinic Assistant

Groups common challenges into themes and drafts session agendas.

7

Translation Assistant

Professional English↔French for notes, goals, messages and more.

8

Risk & Engagement Monitor

Flags at-risk pairs using activity signals only — never message content.

Built to be trusted

Privacy, security & fairness

Private conversations stay private

Messages between a mentor and mentee are confidential. Administrators can see activity signals — how active a pair is — but never the content of their messages.

Nothing is ever really deleted

Participant work is safely archived rather than erased, with clear data-retention and export rules for each cohort.

The right people see the right things

Every screen and action is checked against a person's role, so information is only ever shown to those who should see it.

Fair, language-first design

French and English are treated equally, the interface meets accessibility standards, and the matching rules are transparent and reviewable.
